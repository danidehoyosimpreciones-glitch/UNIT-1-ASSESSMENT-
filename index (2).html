The Refrigerator

The appliance that keeps almost every Colombian home fresh.

Description of its features

  • Keeps food and drinks cold and fresh.
  • Has a freezer for meat, ice and frozen food.
  • Comes in many sizes for every family.
  • No Frost models never need defrosting.
  • Some have a water dispenser and adjustable shelves.

Price

Approximate prices in Colombia (COP):

  • Mini fridge (one door)$600,000 to $1,000,000
  • Basic fridge (Frost)$1,000,000 to $1,400,000
  • Mid-range (No Frost)$1,400,000 to $2,200,000
  • High-end (Side by side)$2,300,000 to $6,000,000+

The price depends on the size, No Frost and Inverter technology.

Features I would add

  • A Wi-Fi app to check the temperature from your phone.
  • A door alarm if it stays open.
  • An energy-saving mode to lower the electricity bill.
  • An easy-to-repair design with cheaper parts.

What I like about it

I like that the refrigerator keeps food fresh and reduces waste.


It also lets families buy more food at once and save money.


Almost every home needs one, so I think it is the most important appliance in the kitchen.

Advantages

  • Keeps food fresh longer
  • Reduces food waste
  • Many sizes and prices
  • No Frost needs no defrosting

Disadvantages

  • Uses electricity all day
  • Can be expensive for some families
  • Takes up space in small kitchens
  • Repairs can be costly
